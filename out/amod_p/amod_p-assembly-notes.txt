AMOD-P -- notes for the assembler
=====================================================

PLACEMENTS
  Use amod_p_cpl_jlcpcb_upload.csv for JLCPCB.  It is amod_p_cpl_kicad_native.csv
  with JLCPCB's own per-part rotations applied; use the native file anywhere else.

SMA jacks (J*) -- straight, through-hole, 5 pins: a square of four legs and a centre pin.  Any quarter turn fits.
DIP switches (SW*) -- the sliders should move toward the side the silkscreen marks ON.
Terminal blocks -- the wire openings face the board's edge.
Diodes and LEDs -- polarised; the cathode is pad 1, at the end the silkscreen bar marks.
Test loops (TP*) -- one pad each; the loop lies along the pad's long axis.
