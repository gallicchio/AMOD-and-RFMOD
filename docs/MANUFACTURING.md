# Manufacturing: ordering the boards at JLCPCB

Generated by `scripts/gen_docs.py` from the fab packages in `out/`.  Rev A, 2026-10-25.  Five boards of each, two
assembled, is the order Appendix D asked for; the cost lines below are the parts of one assembled board at JLCPCB's
unit prices on the day the stock was checked (`scripts/jlc_cache.json` has the time of every answer).

## AMOD-P: the passive board

- Gerbers and drills: `out/amod_p/amod_p-gerbers.zip` (upload as the PCB file).
- Board: 100 x 100 mm, 2 layers, 1.6 mm, JLCPCB's standard stack.
- BOM: `out/amod_p/amod_p_bom_jlcpcb_upload.csv`; placements: `out/amod_p/amod_p_cpl_jlcpcb_upload.csv` (rotations already turned
  for JLCPCB's conventions; the native KiCad file is beside it).
- Assembly: top side only, every part from JLCPCB's shelf, nothing hand-soldered.  42 BOM lines, 31 of them
  extended parts (each adds JLCPCB's loading fee).
- Parts per board: $17.07.  Read `out/amod_p/amod_p-assembly-notes.txt` before confirming the order.

### Order steps

1. Order now > PCB: upload the gerber zip; 100 x 100 mm, 2 layers, 1.6 mm, any colour, HASL or ENIG, 5 pieces.
2. PCB assembly: economic, top side, 2 pieces; upload the BOM and placement files; confirm the parts list (check
   every line against the notes: the DIP switches and the LEDs have printed orientation, the SMA and terminals are
   through-hole and must be selected for assembly).
3. Check the placement preview against `docs/images/amod_p-render-top.png`: polarised parts (diodes, LEDs, ICs' pin 1).
4. Pay; keep the order number in the chat log.
