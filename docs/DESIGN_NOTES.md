- **P8 quartz crystal**: A 4 MHz crystal in series between the jacks: the sharpest resonance on the board.  An HC-49S is 11.5 mm long, more than either quarter of a cell the jacks leave, so it lies at 45 degrees across the cell, from the bottom-left to the top-right, and the two jacks sit 0.5 mm further out along the other diagonal (TLo, BRo) so its courtyard clears theirs.  Each jack's stub runs straight to the crystal's near pad; the loops stand on the stubs, the number in the top-right corner, the name in the bottom-left, the sections in the gutter above.
- **P9 ceramic res.**: A 455 kHz ceramic resonator in series between the jacks, at 45 degrees across the cell (bottom-left to top-right) between the IN jack (top-left) and the OUT jack (bottom-right); each stub runs to the resonator's near lead.
- **P10 clipper**: A 1 k series resistor and two 1N4148W antiparallel to ground: 4.04's clipper.  The diodes stand up from the OUT line into the top-right quarter, either side of the OUT loop's lead, each to its own ground via above it.
- **P11 delay line**: 20 LC sections of 1 uH and 390 pF (50 ohm, 20 ns each): a 400 ns tapped delay line on three cells.  Each section is an 0603 inductor on the line and an 0402 capacitor hanging from the node after it, 4.0 mm a section.  The jacks sit on the checkerboard: IN at the first cell's top-left, OUT at its bottom-right, the 100 and 200 ns taps at the second and third cells' top-left, the 300 ns tap at the third cell's bottom-right.  Row A runs right along the line from IN under the top jacks (twelve sections), drops left of the 300 ns jack, and row B runs back along y = 15.25 (eight) into OUT; row B's capacitors stand up, between row A's.  Each tap leaves its node through 470 ohm: nodes 5 and 10 up past row A to a resistor beside their jack, node 15 down under row B and along the bottom edge into its jack from below.
- **P12 LEDs**: Four LEDs on a 4-way DIP from one jack through 100 ohm: V_f against wavelength is Planck's constant.  The switch stands with its ways as rows (pins 1-4 down its left column), the LEDs in a column to its left at the ways' order, each to its own ground via at the tile's edge; the LED loop sits in the empty top-right slot.
- **P13 AM loopstick**: An AM loopstick: a 3-way terminal for the ferrite rod's coil (ends and tap), 47 / 100 / 220 / 470 pF across it on a 4-way DIP, the jack on the tap; the rod lies along the board's bottom edge under two pairs of cable-tie slots.  The terminal's pins are named in the margin below it, where the wires come in.

## AMOD-A: the active board

12 tiles, 19 jacks, 183 parts.  AMOD-A: the active board.  Sixteen cells, twelve tiles (PLAN.md section 5), four layers: L1 the parts and the drawn wires, L2 ground, L3 the rails as pours (+5 V everywhere, -5 V and +3.3 V in the gutters the tiles reach with a via), L4 ground fill with the Pmod lines and the back-side text.  Every powered tile has the standard header (scripts/tiles.power_header): a slide switch along its left edge and a P-FET between its jacks, so the tile's own rail is dead until the slider is up.  The dense tiles let scripts/router.py find their wires (Tile.route); the silk draws whatever it found, as it draws every hand-laid wire.

- **A1 power**: Power: USB-C on the board's right edge (CC pull-downs ask for 5 V), a 2 A PTC, a 5 V TVS, 2 x 10 uF; the LM2664 inverts +5 to -5 V (40 mA) and the TPS7A2033 regulates +3.3 V; a green LED on +5 V and a red one on -5 V; a 1 x 5 socket with every rail along the top edge, its pins named in the gutter above.  The rails leave on the inner layer: +5 V through a via into the pour that covers the board, -5 V through vias into the strip in the gutter above, +3.3 V through a via into the strip in the gutter below.
- **A2 x10**: Gain x10: an OPA810 (140 MHz GBW) non-inverting, 1.1 k and 10 k, 50 ohm in, 49.9 ohm out (x10 into a high impedance, x5 into 50 ohm); 14 MHz.
- **A3 x100**: Gain x100 in one OPA810 stage (100 ohm and 10 k): 1.4 MHz, x100 into a high impedance.  The plan's two stages of x10 (9 MHz) did not fit a 20 mm cell beside the switch with 0.2 mm clearances; A4 covers the HF case at x100.
- **A4 LNA**: HF LNA: the OPA847 (SOT-23-6) at x101 (1 k / 10 ohm) in the shared checkerboard geometry (opa_tile_br), 50 ohm at the input, 49.9 ohm out.  DIS (pin 5) is tied to +VS beside it: enabled.  The feedback pair sits right at the pins, as a decompensated amplifier needs.
- **A5 noise**: Johnson noise: the source resistor (10 k or 1 k by a 2-way DIP, or your own in the 1 x 2 socket) at the +IN of an LT6230 (1.1 nV/rtHz) at x100 (1 k / 10 ohm) in the A2 geometry, 49.9 ohm out.  No IN jack: the socket and DIP sit in its place.  The amplifier's 2 MHz is the noise bandwidth; the bias current (600 nA) makes 10 k a sensible upper limit (0.6 V of DC at the output).
- **A6 1-bit ADC**: Delta-sigma: a TLV3501 compares the integrator node (IN through 10 k, the FPGA's bit back through 10 k, 10 nF to ground) with a mid-rail reference (10 k / 10 k / 100 n from VS); its bit goes to Pmod IO7 and a loop; the FPGA's bit comes in on Pmod IO8.  +3.3 V so the bit is the FPGA's logic level.
- **A7 PLL 4046**: PLL: a 74HC4046A in TSSOP-16 (rot 180: VCOin, DEMout, R1, R2, PC2, SIGin, PC3, VCC down the left column; GND, C1B, C1A, INH, VCOout, COMPin, PC1, PCP down the right) in the top-right quarter, between the diagonal jacks.  IN through 100 n and 1 k (below the IN jack) into SIGin (self-biased); the VCO (R1 10 k, C1 1 nF: about 100 kHz) out through 470 ohm (in the right strip, beside C1) to the OUT jack and back into COMPin; PC2's tri-state output up through the chip's middle to the 10 k / 100 n lag filter above it, into VCOin.  No loops: probe pin 9 (VCOin, the demodulated FM) and pin 13.
- **A8 heat**: Heater and thermometer: two 15 ohm 2512 in series across VS (0.83 W at 5 V), stacked in the top-left, switched by an AO3400 beside them from Pmod IO6 (10 k pull-down); a TMP235 (10 mV/K + 500 mV) straight above the OUT jack, its output dropping into the jack's pin.  (The plan's NTC divider did not fit beside the two 2512s.)
- **A9 mixer**: Mixer: the MC1496 (SOIC-14 across the cell, rot 90: SIG+, GAIN, GAIN, SIG-, BIAS, OUT+, NC along the bottom; VEE, NC, OUT-, NC, CAR-, NC, CAR+ along the top) on +-5 V.  RF IN through 100 n into SIG+, 51 ohm from each signal input to ground; LO IN through 100 n into CAR+, 51 ohm from each carrier input to a 2.5 V bias (10 k / 10 k / 100 n); 1 k between the gain pins, 3.9 k from BIAS to -5 V (1 mA), 1.5 k loads to the switched 5 V; OUT+ through 100 n to IF OUT (high impedance: 1.5 k source).  The board's Pmod lines pass down the back of this tile's middle x 14 ... 16.5, which the layout keeps free there.  No carrier-null trimmer.
- **A10 light**: Light, two cells, +-5 V.  Left: the PD70 photodiode (reverse-biased from VS) into an OPA810 transimpedance stage (10 k, 10 pF: 1.6 MHz) and out through 49.9 ohm to PD OUT (the TL jack).  Right: DRIVE IN (the TR jack) through 1 k into an OPA810 voltage-to-current source (22 ohm sense: 45 mA per volt) driving the side-view red LED at the board's right edge; a 2N7002 from Pmod IO5 (10 k pull-down) grounds the input for TTL modulation.  The plan's laser terminal and its DIP did not fit beside the jack: solder a module's leads to the LED's pads instead.
- **A11 ultrasound**: 40 kHz ultrasound, two cells.  Left: the DRV8837 H-bridge (IN1, IN2 from Pmod IO4 and IO3) drives a transducer in pins 1 and 5 of a 1 x 5 socket along the top edge; the Pmod plug hangs off the bottom edge below this cell, and the board's Pmod lines come down the back of this cell's right third to it.  Right: the second transducer in its socket, 10 nF into the A2 geometry at x100 (100 ohm / 10 k, 3.9 k to ground at +IN) and out to RX OUT at the right cell's bottom-right jack. +-5 V (the op amp), the bridge on the switched +5 V.
- **A12 audio**: Audio, two cells.  Left: a PAM8302A class-D amplifier (SOIC-8, rot 270: IN-, IN+, BYP, SD along the top; VO+, VDD, GND, VO- along the bottom) driving a speaker on the screw terminal in the right cell's top-left; its input is AUDIO IN through 1 u and the one-bit stream from Pmod IO2 through 10 k into 10 n.  Right: an electret in the 1 x 2 socket (2.2 k bias from VS), 1 u into the A2 geometry at x100 and out to MIC OUT.  +-5 V (the op amp; the class-D runs on the switched +5 V).  No 3.5 mm jack: the one-bit stream is heard on the speaker.

## RF-MOD: the RF board

10 tiles, 23 jacks, 166 parts.  RF-MOD: 915 MHz and 2.4 GHz, rev A.  Four layers: L1 the parts, the drawn wires and, on the RF tiles, a ground pour 0.25 mm from every 50 ohm line (coplanar waveguide over L2); L2 ground; L3 the rails (+5 V everywhere, +7.8 V in the gutter under row 1); L4 ground with the back-side text.  Every jack of an RF tile has a launch void under its centre pin (no copper on L2-L4 within 2.2 mm, the top pour 1.9 mm away) and every 50 ohm line a fence of ground vias.  Every tile keeps IN at the top-left slot and takes OUT at the bottom-right slot (not top-right as the AMOD boards do): the jacks then form a checkerboard whose closest pair is 14.85 mm apart instead of 10.5, room for fingers and a wrench.  Rev A builds what JLCPCB had in stock on 2026-10-25: a splitter (R1), the two filters (R2, R4), the QPL9547 LNA (R5) and a GALI-84+ gain block (R12), the power and reference tile (R9), two ADL5350 mixers (R7, R8), a MAX2871 synthesizer (R10, which carries the Pmod plug) and the TRF3722 quadrature transmitter (R13: Jason's "Yes. Add that tile.", Prompt 13). The printed antennas, the RFX2401C front end and the LTC5510 mixers stayed out.

- **R1 splitter**: A resistive three-port splitter and combiner: 18 ohm from each jack to one node (50/3 ohm would be exact), 6 dB from any port to any other, matched at every port, DC to 6 GHz.  One reference or LO feeds two tiles; two tones combine for an intermodulation test on a gain block; a 6 dB pad between any two ports.  The 915 MHz antenna the plan put here is rev B (the handbook says why).
- **R2 2.45 GHz filter**: 2.45 GHz band-pass: Murata LFB212G45SG8A127 LTCC (2.0 x 1.25 mm), 1.7 dB loss, 2.4-2.5 GHz, 50 ohm both sides; pads 4 in, 2 out, 1 and 3 ground (as JLCPCB numbers them).
- **R4 915 MHz filter**: 915 MHz band-pass: RF360 B39921B2672P810 SAW (1.4 x 1.1 mm, 5 pads), 902-928 MHz, 1.1 dB loss; pad 1 in, 3 out, 2, 4 and 5 ground.  The output pad sits 0.29 mm above the line: a short 45 degree jog brings it back.
