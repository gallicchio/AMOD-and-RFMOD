## AMOD-N: the second passive board

11 tiles, 28 jacks, 84 parts.  AMOD-N: the second passive board, rev A (Prompt 17).  Eleven tiles on sixteen cells: the RF and analog blocks the other boards lacked or dropped, every one passive except two that run on the Pmod's own 3.3 V (no USB): a step attenuator set by hand on a switch or by the FPGA over three Pmod lines, and a one-transistor oscillator.  Prompt 18 gave the attenuator a second cell for its switch and dropped P15 (two Smith-chart loads): n1's 25 ohm standard and n11's switched loads cover the Smith chart.  Jason, Prompt 17: "It seems like there is enough pressure that I should make another analog board.  We should really call this the 2nd board because it's so much simpler than even the active board ... All of the things that you suggested along with some of the things that we've dropped should go on this new analog board ... SLOT for VNA-type calibration, passive splitters" and, mid-turn, "If something can run on 3.3V, it could still be plugged into the PMOD port but wouldn't require an external USB 5V input."  Four layers on JLC04161H-7628 as RF-MOD: L1 the parts and the drawn wires with a ground pour 0.25 mm from every 50 ohm line (coplanar waveguide over L2), L2 ground, L3 the Pmod's 3.3 V under the whole board, L4 ground with the back-side text and the Pmod lines.  Every jack has RF-MOD's launch (no plane under its centre pin) except the calibration SHORT, whose pin is the pour.

- **n1 calibration**: Calibration standards for a vector network analyzer (Jason: "SLOT for VNA-type calibration"): SHORT, OPEN and LOAD at the jacks' pins, a 25 ohm check standard, and a THRU.  Calibrating here puts the reference plane at the board's surface, so every other tile is measured from its pad with its own jack de-embedded (the jacks are all the same part). SHORT: the centre pin is ground, joined all round to the top pour (no launch void).  OPEN: the bare pin with its launch void.  LOAD: 4 mm of 50 ohm line into two 100 ohm in parallel (a matched line is still a match).  25 OHM: two 49.9 ohm at the end of 4.75 mm of line (VSWR 2, a known mismatch to check a calibration with).  THRU: 19.8 mm of line between two jacks.
- **n2 resistive splitter**: A resistive splitter: three 16.9 ohm in a star (50 / 3), every port 50 ohm, 6 dB from any port to either other, DC to 6 GHz, no isolation between the outputs (6 dB of it).  IN at the right cell's top-left, OUT 1 and OUT 2 at the two bottom corners, the star between them so both outputs' lines are the same length (8.7 + 4.65 mm).
- **n3 transformer splitter**: A transformer splitter: Mini-Circuits ADP-2-1+ (case CD636, 0.5-400 MHz, 3 dB plus 0.3, 25 dB between the outputs), turned 270 at the tile's middle so SUM faces the top jack and ports 1 and 2 the bottom corners on lines of the same length; pins 2 and 5 joined by a track under the body and kept off ground, as Mini-Circuits' layout PL-116 does.
- **n4 quarter-wave stub**: A quarter-wave open stub on the line from IN to OUT: 17.3 mm of the same coplanar waveguide, open at its end, a short circuit at the junction at 2.45 GHz (and 7.35 GHz): a notch.  The line's effective permittivity (about 3.1 on JLC04161H-7628) puts the notch within a few percent of 2.45 GHz; measure where it is.
- **n5 low-pass**: A low-pass: Mini-Circuits LFCN-1000+ (LTCC, 1206), DC-1000 MHz, 3 dB at 1.3 GHz, 30 dB from 1.9 to 5 GHz: the harmonics of a 915 MHz synthesizer off, and a clean LO.
- **n6 detector**: A diode detector: 51 ohm across IN (the match), 10 nF in series, a Skyworks SMS7630-006LF pair as a voltage doubler (pin 2, an anode, on ground; pin 3, the joint, the RF; pin 1, a cathode, the output), 100 pF and 100 k on the output. Zero-bias Schottky: square law below about -20 dBm (the output in volts goes as the power), linear above (as the amplitude); 0.9 us of video time constant, so an AM envelope or a pulse comes through.
- **n7 bias-T**: A bias-T: RF in on the top-left jack through 100 nF to RF+DC on the bottom-right, DC fed onto that line through 27 nH, 330 nH and 10 uH in series (the 27 nH nearest the line, for the GHz end) from a test loop (a bench supply's clip, the GND loop beside it), with 100 nF across the DC.  For an active antenna, an LNA at the end of a cable, a varactor or a detector diode's bias.
- **n8 step attenuator**: A digital step attenuator: pSemi PE43711 (QFN-24 4 x 4), 9 kHz-6 GHz, 0-31.75 dB in 0.25 dB steps, set by hand or by the FPGA (Jason, Prompt 18: "If the FPGA pins are high impedance, this would be nice to do manually").  An 8-way DIP switch above the chip: way 1 is SPI (P/S: off = parallel, the chip's "direct parallel" mode with LE held high by 100 k, which pSemi calls "ideal for manual control ... switches or jumpers"; on = serial), ways 2-8 the seven bits 0.25-16 dB (on = that much more attenuation), each pulled down by 100 k standing in the gap beside its line.  In serial mode the seven bits must be off (the datasheet: the parallel inputs grounded), and SDATA, SCLK and LE come from Pmod IO2, IO3 and IO4 (RF-MOD's SPI lines).  Either way the chip needs the Pmod's 3.3 V (150 uA): it is an active switch array. 100 nF blocks at both RF pins (they must sit at 0 V).  Two cells: IN at the left cell's top-left, OUT at the right cell's bottom-right, the chip between them; the plug below the left cell.
- **n9 oscillator**: A one-transistor oscillator on the Pmod's 3.3 V (switched): a Clapp (series-tuned Colpitts) on an MMBT3904 as an emitter follower, 10 uH in series with a BB133 varactor from the base (through 1 nF) to ground, 220 pF base to emitter and 220 pF emitter to ground, 100 k from the supply to the base (about 1.5 mA), 680 ohm in the emitter.  The varactor's cathode sits at (3.3 V + TUNE) / 2 through two 100 k, so TUNE's -2..+2 V moves it from 9.6 to 11.5 MHz (about 10.5 MHz at 0 V); 2.2 pF from TUNE to the same node lets a tone near the oscillator's frequency pull it into lock.  OUT through 22 pF from the emitter (about -22 dBm into 50 ohm).  The transistor stands over the switch's FET (collector down, to the supply); the tank fills the top-right quarter; the supply reaches the bias and the tank, and the output reaches the OUT jack's centre pin, on the back.
- **n10 1:1 transformer**: A 1:1 transformer (Mini-Circuits TC1-1TG2+, 0.4-500 MHz, separate windings) in the top-right quarter: IN over its top onto the primary, the secondary down onto the OUT jack, whose four legs are a ground of their own (an island with no plane within 5.6 mm), so OUT floats.  A balun (OUT's centre and shell are the two halves of a dipole, the tutorial's on-the-air feed) and a ground-loop breaker between an instrument and the host.  The centre tap is left open.
- **n11 1:4 transformer**: A 1:4 transformer (Mini-Circuits TC4-1W+, 3-800 MHz) in the top-right quarter: IN on the primary, the secondary from ground to OUT, so OUT's impedance is four times IN's and its voltage twice.  A DIP switch in the bottom-left quarter puts 200 ohm (IN then reads 50) or 49.9 ohm (IN reads 12.5) across OUT: the turns ratio squared, measured.

## AMOD-A: the active board

12 tiles, 21 jacks, 185 parts.  AMOD-A: the active board.  Sixteen cells, twelve tiles (PLAN.md section 5), four layers: L1 the parts and the drawn wires, L2 ground, L3 the rails as pours (+5 V everywhere, -5 V and +3.3 V in the gutters the tiles reach with a via), L4 ground fill with the Pmod lines and the back-side text.  Every powered tile has the standard header (scripts/tiles.power_header): a slide switch along its left edge and a P-FET between its jacks, so the tile's own rail is dead until the slider is up.  The dense tiles let scripts/router.py find their wires (Tile.route); the silk draws whatever it found, as it draws every hand-laid wire.

- **a1 power**: 
- **a2 x10**: Gain x10: an OPA810 (140 MHz GBW) non-inverting, 1.1 k and 10 k, 50 ohm in, 49.9 ohm out (x10 into a high impedance, x5 into 50 ohm); 14 MHz.
- **a3 x100**: Gain x100 in one OPA810 stage (100 ohm and 10 k): 1.4 MHz, x100 into a high impedance.  The plan's two stages of x10 (9 MHz) did not fit a 20 mm cell beside the switch with 0.2 mm clearances; a4 covers the HF case at x100.
- **a4 LNA**: HF LNA: the OPA847 (SOT-23-6) at x101 (1 k / 10 ohm) in the shared checkerboard geometry (opa_tile_br), 50 ohm at the input, 49.9 ohm out.  DIS (pin 5) is tied to +VS beside it: enabled.  The feedback pair sits right at the pins, as a decompensated amplifier needs.
- **a5 Doppler**: 10.525 GHz Doppler radar: an HB100 module on three jumper wires to a 1 x 3 header (+5 V from the tile's switch, IF, ground), its IF through 1 uF (100 k to ground) into the a2 geometry at x101 (100 ohm / 10 k), 49.9 ohm out.  1 m/s toward the module is 70 Hz; walking, a fan's blades, a speaker cone and breathing are all within reach.  (Rev A's first a5 was a Johnson-noise amplifier: the ECP5-2ADC-2DAC board has that source and a x100 input of its own.)
- **a6 1-bit ADC**: Delta-sigma: a TLV3501 compares the integrator node (IN through 10 k, the FPGA's bit back through 10 k, 10 nF to ground) with a mid-rail reference (10 k / 10 k / 100 n from VS); its bit goes to Pmod IO7 and a loop; the FPGA's bit comes in on Pmod IO8.  +3.3 V so the bit is the FPGA's logic level.
- **a7 PLL 4046**: PLL: a 74HC4046A in TSSOP-16 (rot 180: VCOin, DEMout, R1, R2, PC2, SIGin, PC3, VCC down the left column; GND, C1B, C1A, INH, VCOout, COMPin, PC1, PCP down the right) in the top-right quarter, between the diagonal jacks.  IN through 100 n and 1 k (below the IN jack) into SIGin (self-biased); the VCO (R1 10 k, C1 1 nF: about 100 kHz) out through 470 ohm (in the right strip, beside C1) to the OUT jack and back into COMPin; PC2's tri-state output up through the chip's middle to the 10 k / 100 n lag filter above it, into VCOin.  No loops: probe pin 9 (VCOin, the demodulated FM) and pin 13.
- **a8 heat**: Heater and thermometer: two 15 ohm 2512 in series across VS (0.83 W at 5 V), stacked in the top-left, switched by an AO3400 beside them from Pmod IO6 (10 k pull-down); a TMP235 (10 mV/K + 500 mV) straight above the OUT jack, its output dropping into the jack's pin.  (The plan's NTC divider did not fit beside the two 2512s.)
- **a9 mixer**: Mixer: the MC1496 (SOIC-14 across the cell, rot 90: SIG+, GAIN, GAIN, SIG-, BIAS, OUT+, NC along the bottom; VEE, NC, OUT-, NC, CAR-, NC, CAR+ along the top) on +-5 V.  RF IN through 100 n into SIG+, 51 ohm from each signal input to ground; LO IN through 100 n into CAR+, 51 ohm from each carrier input to a 2.5 V bias (10 k / 10 k / 100 n); 1 k between the gain pins, 3.9 k from BIAS to -5 V (1 mA), 1.5 k loads to the switched 5 V; OUT+ through 100 n to IF OUT (high impedance: 1.5 k source).  The board's Pmod lines pass down the back of this tile's middle x 14 ... 16.5, which the layout keeps free there.  No carrier-null trimmer.
- **a10 impedance**: Impedance and I-V, two cells, +-5 V.  Left: DRIVE IN (50 ohm) into the a2 geometry at x2 (1 k / 1 k): the driven node S, which also leaves through 49.9 ohm on S OUT (the bottom-right jack).  Right: a 4-way DIP picks the reference resistor (47, 470, 4.7 k, 47 k) from S to X, the part under test between X and ground on the DUT jack, and an OPA810 follower brings X out through 49.9 ohm on X OUT.  With the host's two ADCs on S OUT and X OUT (50 ohm: each reads half), I = (S - X) / R and Z = R X / (S - X): an impedance analyzer from DC to a few MHz after an open-short calibration, and an I-V curve tracer to +-4 V and 40 mA.  (Rev A's first a10 was an LED driver and a photodiode amplifier: the ECP5-2ADC-2DAC board has both.)
- **a11 ultrasound**: 40 kHz ultrasound, two cells.  Left: the DRV8837 H-bridge (IN1, IN2 from Pmod IO4 and IO3) drives a transducer in pins 1 and 5 of a 1 x 5 socket along the top edge; the Pmod plug hangs off the bottom edge below this cell, and the board's Pmod lines come down the back of this cell's right third to it.  Right: the second transducer in its socket, 10 nF into the a2 geometry at x100 (100 ohm / 10 k, 3.9 k to ground at +IN) and out to RX OUT at the right cell's bottom-right jack. +-5 V (the op amp), the bridge on the switched +5 V.
- **a12 audio**: Audio, two cells.  Left: a PAM8302A class-D amplifier (SOIC-8, rot 270: IN-, IN+, BYP, SD along the top; VO+, VDD, GND, VO- along the bottom) driving a speaker on the screw terminal in the right cell's top-left; its input is SPEAKER IN through 1 u and the one-bit stream from Pmod IO2 through 10 k into 10 n.  Right: an electret in the 1 x 2 socket (2.2 k bias from VS), 1 u into the a2 geometry at x100 and out to MIC OUT.  +-5 V (the op amp; the class-D runs on the switched +5 V).  No 3.5 mm jack: the one-bit stream is heard on the speaker.

## AMOD-C: the conversion board

12 tiles, 26 jacks, 207 parts.  AMOD-C: conversion, rev A (Prompt 19).  Sixteen cells, four layers, USB-C power on c1 as AMOD-A's a1.  Power conversion (a buck and an LDO under an electronic load, a charge pump the FPGA clocks) and data conversion (a second-order delta-sigma with its integrators on jacks, a track-and-hold, a comparator that makes the host's DAC a SAR or tracking ADC), the switched capacitors both use (a filter chip and a capacitor-and-two-switches resistor), and the component-level stages Analog Devices' university labs teach (a differential pair on a current mirror, common emitter against cascode, a precision rectifier, a 555).  `docs/ADI_LABS_SURVEY.md` has the survey; PLAN.md sections 12 and 13 the decisions.  Jason, Prompt 18: "Whole areas that we are missing include power regulation and other component-level circuits, but something like a switched capacitor filter or details of their Delta - Sigma Modulator, if there is room, might be worth it."  L1 the parts and the drawn wires, L2 ground, L3 the rails (+5 V under the board; -5 V in the gutter under row 0, down the left margin and along the bottom margin; +3.3 V in the gutters under rows 1 and 2, joined down the right margin), L4 ground with the Pmod lines and the back-side text.  Every powered tile has the standard header (tiles.power_header): a slide switch and a P-FET, so its rail is dead until the slider is up.

- **c1 power**: 
- **c2 regulators**: A buck converter, an LDO after it and an electronic load, two cells.  Left: the TLV62568 (1.5 MHz, pulse-skipping at light load) from the switched 5 V with 2.2 uH, 10 uF in and out, its feedback 100 k / 20 k (3.6 V) and TRIM (a DAC) through 100 k into the feedback node, so the buck's output moves 2.2-5 V and the LDO can be pushed into dropout; BUCK~ is its output AC-coupled (1 uF, 49.9 ohm) for the ripple.  Right: the TPS7A2033 LDO (3.3 V) from the buck, LDO~ its output AC-coupled likewise; an OPA810 holds 10 ohm at LOAD's voltage through an AO3400A: 100 mA per volt from the LDO's output (LOAD terminated in 49.9 ohm), its loop closed slowly through 1 k and 1 nF so the FET's gate cannot make it ring.  The buck (SOT-23-5 at rot 0: EN, GND, SW down its left; VIN, FB up its right) in the left cell's top-right quarter, EN to VIN under its body, the inductor under SW, the feedback network stacked right of FB; its output along y 9.8 to BUCK~'s coupling in the gutter and on the back to the LDO in the right cell's top-right quarter (turned 0: IN, GND, EN down its left; OUT up its right), whose output goes right and down to LDO~'s coupling over its jack and left and down to the load in the right cell's bottom-left quarter.
- **c3 charge pump**: A charge pump the FPGA clocks: the ICL7660A from the switched 5 V, its OSC pin driven from Pmod IO6 through an SN74LV1T34 and 1 k (the pump runs at half the clock), 1 uF flying and 1 uF on the output, 1 k of load on the output; CAP shows the flying capacitor's top (0-5 V) and OUT the output (about -5 V), each through 47 k / 10 k (read at high impedance).  SOIC-8 at rot 0 (BOOST, CAP+, GND, CAP- down its left at 11.925; V+, OSC, LV, VOUT down its right at 16.875) in the top-right quarter: the flying capacitor standing left of it with CAP's 47 k above, the output capacitor, V+'s capacitor and the load down the right edge, OSC under the body to the 1 k from the buffer below; CAP's 10 k under its jack, OUT's divider over the OUT jack.  VS comes up the back to the buffer and V+; IO6 from the back.
- **c4 SC filter**: A switched-capacitor filter: the MAX7410 (5th-order Butterworth, fc = fCLK / 100, 5 V) clocked from Pmod IO3 through an SN74LV1T34 (the clock pin wants 4.5 V); IN terminated in 49.9 ohm and AC-coupled into the chip's input, biased at COM (2.5 V) by 100 k; OS on COM; OUT AC-coupled through 1 uF and 49.9 ohm to the OUT jack.  The chip at rot 0 (COM, IN, GND, VDD down its left at 12.8; CLK, SHDN, OS, OUT down its right at 17.2), the buffer above its right side, VS up the back along the tile's right edge to SHDN and the buffer.
- **c5 SC integrator**: A switched-capacitor resistor and integrator, two cells along the top: a TMUX4053 (three SPDTs on +-5 V, 3.3 V logic) switches 1 nF between IN and ground on one side (phi1, Pmod IO4) and between ground and the integrator's summing node on the other (phi2, IO8): the parasitic-insensitive integrator, whose resistor is 1 / (f C).  An OPA810 with 10 nF (C0G) and 1 M across it integrates; OUT through 49.9 ohm.  The FPGA makes the two phases, so it can make them overlap on purpose.  The third switch is grounded.  The chip turned 180 in the right cell's top-left quarter (SEL3, SEL2, SEL1, S1A, S1B, D1, D2, VDD down its left at 24.14; GND, VSS, EN, S3A, D3, S3B, S2A, S2B down its right at 29.86), its grounds joined under its body; IN along y 4.75 from the jack; the capacitor standing left of D1 and D2.  The integrator below (the OPA810 at rot 270: +IN, V-, OUT along its top, -IN and V+ along its bottom), the summing node coming down from S2B to a column on its left, the feedback pair lying under it, OUT over to the right and on to the jack between its top legs.  VS up the back from the header; -5 V on the back to the right edge and the gutter.
- **c6 delta-sigma**: A second-order delta-sigma modulator with its insides on jacks, two cells.  Both OPA2810 integrators sit on a 1.65 V virtual ground (VMID: the board's +3.3 V halved by two 0.1 % 10 k), so each one-bit DAC is just an SN74LVC1G3157 switching 10 k between +3.3 V and ground: +-165 uA about VMID, ratiometric with VMID, with no negative reference; 10 k from +3.3 V into the first node cancels IN's own offset from VMID.  IN through 10 k into 220 pF, the first output through 6.8 k into 330 pF; a TLV3501 compares the second output with VMID; a 74LVC1G79 registers it on the FPGA's clock (Pmod IO1) and hands the bit to IO7 and to both switches (the first wired the other way round, so both loops are negative).  INT1 and INT2 on their jacks through 3.3 k / 1 k (read at high impedance: about a quarter, offset by 0.38 V).  Full scale +-1.65 V; clock 1 MHz.  The OPA2810 (SOIC-8 at rot 0: OUT A, -IN A, +IN A, V- down its left at 24.5; V+, OUT B, -IN B, +IN B down its right at 29.5) in the right cell's top-left quarter: the first integrator's parts on a column left of it, the second's right of it; OUT A crosses under the body and out of its bottom to the 6.8 k and INT1's divider; +IN A and +IN B on vias under the body joined on the back, V+ on a via under the body.  The comparator and the register over the right cell's top-right, the second switch under the second integrator, the first switch beside its 10 k; VMID's divider in the right cell's bottom-left quarter.  VS comes along the bottom edge and up the back; the bit and IO1 leave on the back for the Pmod plug.
- **c7 track and hold**: A track-and-hold: the LF398 on +-5 V (its minimum), 1 nF C0G as the hold capacitor, LOGIC from Pmod IO5 (high: track; TTL threshold 1.4 V, so 3.3 V drives it), LOGIC REF grounded; IN terminated in 49.9 ohm, OUT through 49.9 ohm. The SOIC-14 turned 90 low in the cell (IN, V-, OUT along its bottom at y 10.08; OFFSET, V+, LOGIC, LOGIC REF, CH along its top at 5.13), the hold capacitor, V+'s capacitor and the logic's via in the band above it; IN down the left to pin 1, its termination below; OUT round the right edge to its 49.9 ohm and in between the jack's right legs; V- to a via under the body and on the back to its capacitor in the strip left of the jack and the bottom margin; VS up the back on the left.
- **c8 comparator**: A comparator for a successive-approximation or tracking ADC whose DAC is the host's: a TLV3201 on +3.3 V (40 ns, 1.2 mV of hysteresis: the TLV3501's 6 mV would be 12 LSB at 12 bits) compares IN with DAC, each averaged with the 3.3 V rail through a matched pair of 0.1 % 10 k (so both sit at 1.15-2.15 V inside the comparator's range and the comparison is unchanged), its decision to Pmod IO2 and a loop.  The gateware runs the search: set the DAC, read the bit, halve the step.
- **c9 differential pair**: A differential pair on a current mirror, two cells along the bottom: a matched NPN pair (DMMT3904W) with IN+ (DAC 1) and IN- (DAC 2) on the bases, each terminated in 49.9 ohm, their emitters on a 1 mA tail (an MMBT3904 with its base at -3.4 V from 6.8 k / 3.3 k and 1 k to -5 V); a matched PNP pair (BCM857BS) mirrors the left collector's current onto the right one, so the output node carries Ic1 - Ic2, which an OPA810 transimpedance stage (1 k) turns into OUT: tanh of the input difference over 2 VT, +-1 V at the tail's full 1 mA.  The mirror (SOT-363 turned 180: E2, B2, C1 down its left; C2, B1, E1 down its right) over the pair (rot 0: E1, B1, C2 down its left; C1, B2, E2 down its right) in the left cell's top-right quarter, the inputs coming down to the pair's bases from either side; the mirror's emitters joined under its body, the pair's likewise; the mirror's diode node in the channel between the two; the tail standing under the pair with its divider and emitter resistor below.
