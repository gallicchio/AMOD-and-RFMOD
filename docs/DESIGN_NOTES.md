- **P11 delay line**: 20 LC sections of 1 uH and 390 pF (50 ohm, 20 ns each): a 400 ns tapped delay line on three cells. - **P13 AM loopstick**: An AM loopstick: a 3-way terminal for the ferrite rod's coil (ends and tap), 47 / 100 / 220 / 470 pF across it on a 4-way DIP, the jack on the tap; the rod lies along the board's bottom edge under two pairs of cable-tie slots.  The terminal's pins are named in the margin below it, where the wires come in.

## AMOD-A: the active board

12 tiles, 19 jacks, 182 parts.  AMOD-A: the active board.  Sixteen cells, twelve tiles (PLAN.md section 5), four layers: L1 the parts and the drawn wires, L2 ground, L3 the rails as pours (+5 V everywhere, -5 V and +3.3 V in the gutters the tiles reach with a via), L4 ground fill with the Pmod lines and the back-side text.  Every powered tile has the standard header (scripts/tiles.power_header): a slide switch along its left edge and a P-FET between its jacks, so the tile's own rail is dead until the slider is up.  The dense tiles let scripts/router.py find their wires (Tile.route); the silk draws whatever it found, as it draws every hand-laid wire.

- **A1 power**: Power: USB-C on the board's right edge (CC pull-downs ask for 5 V), a 2 A PTC, a 5 V TVS, 2 x 10 uF; the LM2664 inverts +5 to -5 V (40 mA) and the TPS7A2033 regulates +3.3 V; a green LED on +5 V and a red one on -5 V; a 1 x 5 socket with every rail along the top edge, its pins named in the gutter above.  The rails leave on the inner layer: +5 V through a via into the pour that covers the board, -5 V through vias into the strip in the gutter above, +3.3 V through a via into the strip in the gutter below.
- **A2 x10**: Gain x10: an OPA810 (140 MHz GBW) non-inverting, 1.1 k and 10 k, 50 ohm in, 49.9 ohm out (x10 into a high impedance, x5 into 50 ohm); 14 MHz.
- **A3 x100**: Gain x100 in one OPA810 stage (100 ohm and 10 k): 1.4 MHz, x100 into a high impedance.  The plan's two stages of x10 (9 MHz) did not fit a 20 mm cell beside the switch with 0.2 mm clearances; A4 covers the HF case at x100.
- **A4 LNA**: HF LNA: the OPA847 (SOIC-8, rot 90: DIS, V+, OUT, NC along the top, NC, -IN, +IN, V- along the bottom) at x100 (1 k / 10 ohm), 50 ohm at the input, 49.9 ohm out.  The output goes over the top to Rf standing left of the chip; the feedback returns along the bottom through pin 1 (NC on the OPA847, so it may carry the node) into -IN; the input comes under Rg to +IN.  VS reaches the top row's DIS pin and the decoupling capacitor on the back. No test loop: the cell is full (the OUT jack is the monitor).
- **A5 noise**: Johnson noise: the source resistor (10 k or 1 k by a 2-way DIP, or your own in the 1 x 2 socket) at the +IN of an LT6230 (1.1 nV/rtHz) at x100 (1 k / 10 ohm) in the A2 geometry, 49.9 ohm out.  No IN jack: the socket and DIP sit in its place.  The amplifier's 2 MHz is the noise bandwidth; the bias current (600 nA) makes 10 k a sensible upper limit (0.6 V of DC at the output).
- **A6 1-bit ADC**: Delta-sigma: a TLV3501 compares the integrator node (IN through 10 k, the FPGA's bit back through 10 k, 10 nF to ground) with a mid-rail reference (10 k / 10 k / 100 n from VS); its bit goes to Pmod 2 and a loop; the FPGA's bit comes in on Pmod 1.  +3.3 V so the bit is the FPGA's logic level.
- **A7 PLL 4046**: PLL: a CD74HC4046A (SOIC-16 lying across the cell, rot 270: GND, C1, INH, VCOout, COMPin, PC1, PCP along the top; VCOin, R1, PC2, SIGin, VCC along the bottom).  IN through 100 n and 1 k into SIGin (self-biased); the VCO (R1 10 k, C1 1 nF: about 100 kHz) out through 470 ohm and back into COMPin; PC2's tri-state output through the 10 k / 100 n lag filter into VCOin.  The three wires that must cross the chip run in the gap between its pin rows; the loop filter and the input network stand in the column left of the chip (0402s).  No loops: probe pin 9 (VCOin, the demodulated FM) and pin 13.
- **A8 heat**: Heater and thermometers: two 15 ohm 2512 in series across VS (0.83 W at 5 V) switched by an AO3400 from Pmod 3 (10 k pull-down); a 10 k NTC in a divider with 10 k to a loop; a TMP235 (10 mV/K + 500 mV) to the OUT jack.  The heaters lie in the middle of the cell, the NTC against their left pads.
- **A9 mixer**: Mixer: the MC1496 (SOIC-14 across the cell, rot 90: SIG+, GAIN, GAIN, SIG-, BIAS, OUT+, NC along the bottom; VEE, NC, OUT-, NC, CAR-, NC, CAR+ along the top) on +-5 V.  RF IN through 100 n into SIG+, 51 ohm from each signal input to ground; LO IN through 100 n into CAR+, 51 ohm from each carrier input to a 2.5 V bias (10 k / 10 k / 100 n); 1 k between the gain pins, 3.9 k from BIAS to -5 V (1 mA), 1.5 k loads to the switched 5 V; OUT+ through 100 n to IF OUT (high impedance: 1.5 k source).  The board's Pmod lines pass down the back of this tile's middle x 14 ... 16.5, which the layout keeps free there.  No carrier-null trimmer.
- **A10 light**: Light, two cells, +-5 V.  Left: the PD70 photodiode (reverse-biased from VS) into an OPA810 transimpedance stage (10 k, 10 pF: 1.6 MHz) and out through 49.9 ohm to PD OUT (the TL jack).  Right: DRIVE IN (the TR jack) through 1 k into an OPA810 voltage-to-current source (22 ohm sense: 45 mA per volt) driving the side-view red LED at the board's right edge; a 2N7002 from Pmod 4 (10 k pull-down) grounds the input for TTL modulation.  The plan's laser terminal and its DIP did not fit beside the jack: solder a module's leads to the LED's pads instead.
- **A11 ultrasound**: 40 kHz ultrasound, two cells.  Left: the DRV8837 H-bridge (IN1, IN2 from Pmod 5 and 6) drives a transducer in pins 1 and 5 of a 1 x 5 socket along the top edge; the Pmod plug hangs off the bottom edge below this cell, and the board's Pmod lines come down the back of this cell's right third to it.  Right: the second transducer in its socket, 10 nF into the A2 geometry at x100 (100 ohm / 10 k, 3.9 k to ground at +IN) and out to RX OUT. +-5 V (the op amp), the bridge on the switched +5 V.
- **A12 audio**: Audio, two cells.  Left: a PAM8302A class-D amplifier (SOIC-8, rot 270: IN-, IN+, BYP, SD along the top; VO+, VDD, GND, VO- along the bottom) driving a speaker on the screw terminal in the right cell's top-left; its input is AUDIO IN through 1 u and the one-bit stream from Pmod 7 through 10 k into 10 n.  Right: an electret in the 1 x 2 socket (2.2 k bias from VS), 1 u into the A2 geometry at x100 and out to MIC OUT.  +-5 V (the op amp; the class-D runs on the switched +5 V).  No 3.5 mm jack: the one-bit stream is heard on the speaker.
