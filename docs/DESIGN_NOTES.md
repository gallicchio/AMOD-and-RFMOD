## AMOD-A: the active board

12 tiles, 21 jacks, 185 parts.  AMOD-A: the active board.  Sixteen cells, twelve tiles (PLAN.md section 5), four layers: L1 the parts and the drawn wires, L2 ground, L3 the rails as pours (+5 V everywhere, -5 V and +3.3 V in the gutters the tiles reach with a via), L4 ground fill with the Pmod lines and the back-side text.  Every powered tile has the standard header (scripts/tiles.power_header): a slide switch along its left edge and a P-FET between its jacks, so the tile's own rail is dead until the slider is up.  The dense tiles let scripts/router.py find their wires (Tile.route); the silk draws whatever it found, as it draws every hand-laid wire.

- **A1 power**: Power: USB-C on the board's right edge (CC pull-downs ask for 5 V), a 2 A PTC, a 5 V TVS, 2 x 10 uF; the LM2664 inverts +5 to -5 V (40 mA) and the TPS7A2033 regulates +3.3 V; a green LED on +5 V and a red one on -5 V; a 1 x 5 socket with every rail along the top edge, its pins named in the gutter above.  The rails leave on the inner layer: +5 V through a via into the pour that covers the board, -5 V through vias into the strip in the gutter above, +3.3 V through a via into the strip in the gutter below.
- **A2 x10**: Gain x10: an OPA810 (140 MHz GBW) non-inverting, 1.1 k and 10 k, 50 ohm in, 49.9 ohm out (x10 into a high impedance, x5 into 50 ohm); 14 MHz.
- **A3 x100**: Gain x100 in one OPA810 stage (100 ohm and 10 k): 1.4 MHz, x100 into a high impedance.  The plan's two stages of x10 (9 MHz) did not fit a 20 mm cell beside the switch with 0.2 mm clearances; A4 covers the HF case at x100.
- **A4 LNA**: HF LNA: the OPA847 (SOT-23-6) at x101 (1 k / 10 ohm) in the shared checkerboard geometry (opa_tile_br), 50 ohm at the input, 49.9 ohm out.  DIS (pin 5) is tied to +VS beside it: enabled.  The feedback pair sits right at the pins, as a decompensated amplifier needs.
- **A5 Doppler**: 10.525 GHz Doppler radar: an HB100 module on three jumper wires to a 1 x 3 header (+5 V from the tile's switch, IF, ground), its IF through 1 uF (100 k to ground) into the A2 geometry at x101 (100 ohm / 10 k), 49.9 ohm out.  1 m/s toward the module is 70 Hz; walking, a fan's blades, a speaker cone and breathing are all within reach.  (Rev A's first A5 was a Johnson-noise amplifier: the ECP5-2ADC-2DAC board has that source and a x100 input of its own.)
- **A6 1-bit ADC**: Delta-sigma: a TLV3501 compares the integrator node (IN through 10 k, the FPGA's bit back through 10 k, 10 nF to ground) with a mid-rail reference (10 k / 10 k / 100 n from VS); its bit goes to Pmod IO7 and a loop; the FPGA's bit comes in on Pmod IO8.  +3.3 V so the bit is the FPGA's logic level.
- **A7 PLL 4046**: PLL: a 74HC4046A in TSSOP-16 (rot 180: VCOin, DEMout, R1, R2, PC2, SIGin, PC3, VCC down the left column; GND, C1B, C1A, INH, VCOout, COMPin, PC1, PCP down the right) in the top-right quarter, between the diagonal jacks.  IN through 100 n and 1 k (below the IN jack) into SIGin (self-biased); the VCO (R1 10 k, C1 1 nF: about 100 kHz) out through 470 ohm (in the right strip, beside C1) to the OUT jack and back into COMPin; PC2's tri-state output up through the chip's middle to the 10 k / 100 n lag filter above it, into VCOin.  No loops: probe pin 9 (VCOin, the demodulated FM) and pin 13.
- **A8 heat**: Heater and thermometer: two 15 ohm 2512 in series across VS (0.83 W at 5 V), stacked in the top-left, switched by an AO3400 beside them from Pmod IO6 (10 k pull-down); a TMP235 (10 mV/K + 500 mV) straight above the OUT jack, its output dropping into the jack's pin.  (The plan's NTC divider did not fit beside the two 2512s.)
- **A9 mixer**: Mixer: the MC1496 (SOIC-14 across the cell, rot 90: SIG+, GAIN, GAIN, SIG-, BIAS, OUT+, NC along the bottom; VEE, NC, OUT-, NC, CAR-, NC, CAR+ along the top) on +-5 V.  RF IN through 100 n into SIG+, 51 ohm from each signal input to ground; LO IN through 100 n into CAR+, 51 ohm from each carrier input to a 2.5 V bias (10 k / 10 k / 100 n); 1 k between the gain pins, 3.9 k from BIAS to -5 V (1 mA), 1.5 k loads to the switched 5 V; OUT+ through 100 n to IF OUT (high impedance: 1.5 k source).  The board's Pmod lines pass down the back of this tile's middle x 14 ... 16.5, which the layout keeps free there.  No carrier-null trimmer.
- **A10 impedance**: Impedance and I-V, two cells, +-5 V.  Left: DRIVE IN (50 ohm) into the A2 geometry at x2 (1 k / 1 k): the driven node S, which also leaves through 49.9 ohm on S OUT (the bottom-right jack).  Right: a 4-way DIP picks the reference resistor (47, 470, 4.7 k, 47 k) from S to X, the part under test between X and ground on the DUT jack, and an OPA810 follower brings X out through 49.9 ohm on X OUT.  With the host's two ADCs on S OUT and X OUT (50 ohm: each reads half), I = (S - X) / R and Z = R X / (S - X): an impedance analyzer from DC to a few MHz after an open-short calibration, and an I-V curve tracer to +-4 V and 40 mA.  (Rev A's first A10 was an LED driver and a photodiode amplifier: the ECP5-2ADC-2DAC board has both.)
- **A11 ultrasound**: 40 kHz ultrasound, two cells.  Left: the DRV8837 H-bridge (IN1, IN2 from Pmod IO4 and IO3) drives a transducer in pins 1 and 5 of a 1 x 5 socket along the top edge; the Pmod plug hangs off the bottom edge below this cell, and the board's Pmod lines come down the back of this cell's right third to it.  Right: the second transducer in its socket, 10 nF into the A2 geometry at x100 (100 ohm / 10 k, 3.9 k to ground at +IN) and out to RX OUT at the right cell's bottom-right jack. +-5 V (the op amp), the bridge on the switched +5 V.
- **A12 audio**: Audio, two cells.  Left: a PAM8302A class-D amplifier (SOIC-8, rot 270: IN-, IN+, BYP, SD along the top; VO+, VDD, GND, VO- along the bottom) driving a speaker on the screw terminal in the right cell's top-left; its input is AUDIO IN through 1 u and the one-bit stream from Pmod IO2 through 10 k into 10 n.  Right: an electret in the 1 x 2 socket (2.2 k bias from VS), 1 u into the A2 geometry at x100 and out to MIC OUT.  +-5 V (the op amp; the class-D runs on the switched +5 V).  No 3.5 mm jack: the one-bit stream is heard on the speaker.

## RF-MOD: the RF board

10 tiles, 23 jacks, 166 parts.  RF-MOD: 915 MHz and 2.4 GHz, rev A.  Four layers: L1 the parts, the drawn wires and, on the RF tiles, a ground pour 0.25 mm from every 50 ohm line (coplanar waveguide over L2); L2 ground; L3 the rails (+5 V everywhere, +7.8 V in the gutter under row 1); L4 ground with the back-side text.  Every jack of an RF tile has a launch void under its centre pin (no copper on L2-L4 within 2.2 mm, the top pour 1.9 mm away) and every 50 ohm line a fence of ground vias.  Every tile keeps IN at the top-left slot and takes OUT at the bottom-right slot (not top-right as the AMOD boards do): the jacks then form a checkerboard whose closest pair is 14.85 mm apart instead of 10.5, room for fingers and a wrench. Add that tile.", Prompt 13). The printed antennas, the RFX2401C front end and the LTC5510 mixers stayed out.

## AMOD-2: the second-level HF board

10 tiles, 26 jacks, 232 parts.  AMOD-2: HF, magnetic resonance and the reflectometer, rev A.  The second-level HF board, for the ECP5-2ADC-2DAC board, whose converters sample DC to 30 MHz directly: a 1 W pulse amplifier and a duplexer with a low-noise preamplifier for pulsed NMR, a coil driver for gradients and Helmholtz pairs, a two-channel HF receiver front end, VLF loop amplifiers, a 50 ohm reflection bridge for complex S11, coupled and parametric resonators, a switched noise source and a laser driver with an RF bias-T.  Four layers: L1 parts and drawn wires, L2 ground, L3 the rails (+5 V everywhere, -5 V in the gutter under row 0), L4 ground with the back-side text; +-15 V only reach the pulse amplifier, on the back from Y1 below it.  Designed for Prompt 13 (the second-level boards, for the ECP5-2ADC-2DAC board).  PLAN_LEVEL2.md section 3 has the reasons and the changes from Appendix D's first AMOD-2 (the optics tiles moved to a future board).

- **Y1 power**: Power, two cells, no jacks.  Left: the TPS65131 boost and inverter make +15 and -15 V from the 5 V (1 M / 88.7 k and 1.3 M / 105 k, TI's 4.7 uH and Schottky rectifiers, 3 x 10 uF on each output), carried up the back to Y2's pulse amplifier only.  Right: AMOD-A's USB-C input (5.1 k CC pull-downs, 2 A PTC, 5 V TVS, 2 x 10 uF) and the LM2664 that makes -5 V into the strip in the gutter above; a green LED on +5 V, a red one on -5 V, a yellow one on +15 V.
- **Y2 pulse amplifier**: The pulse amplifier: a THS3491 (VQFN-16 at (12.0, 8.5), rot 0: FB, -IN, +IN down its left, VOUT on its right, +VS on top, -VS along the bottom) at gain 10.3 (698 ohm from its FB pin, which is the output, to -IN; 75 ohm to ground) from +-15 V.  IN is terminated in 49.9 ohm, so a DAC's +-1 V into 50 ohm becomes +-10 V into a 50 ohm load: 1 W.  OUT is not back-terminated (it drives the T/R switch and the probe).  PD from Pmod IO1 through 4.7 k, 10 k to ground: off unless the FPGA holds it high, so it is silent between pulses; it heats if left on at full power (pulse it).  +-15 V come up the back from Y1.
- **Y3 duplexer and preamp**: NMR's duplexer and preamplifier, two cells.  Left: TX IN through two pairs of crossed 1N4148W diodes to PROBE: the pulse passes (1.4 V drop), the transmitter's noise between pulses does not.  Right: LINE (from the probe through a quarter-wave cable, or a short cable and a 6 dB pad) clamped to ground by crossed diodes and terminated in 49.9 ohm, into AMOD-A's op-amp tile with an OPA847 at gain 12 (1.1 k / 100 ohm), 49.9 ohm out to RX OUT.  With the quarter-wave line the clamp shorts its far end during the pulse, so the probe sees an open there; between pulses the echo reaches the preamplifier.
- **Y4 coil driver**: The coil driver, one cell in the board's corner: a DRV8876 H-bridge in PH/EN mode (Pmod IO6 enables and is the PWM, IO7 the direction; 100 k holds both low), nSLEEP and the 5 V divider from the switched rail.  VM through two B5819W: USB's 5 V (4.6 V) or 12-18 V on the right-margin terminal, whichever is higher (the 25 V bulk capacitor sets the limit).  IPROPI into 1.5 k sets the current limit with VREF (5.1 k / 3.3 k: 2 V) and is buffered by an OPA810 (from VM) into MON through 49.9 ohm. The coil on the lower terminal.  The chip at (13, 12.5), rot 0: control pins down its left, the charge pump, VM and OUT2 down its right, OUT1 at its foot; the diodes above it, the buffer beside the MON jack; IO6 / IO7 come down the back at x 9.55 / 8.95 to vias beside pins 1 and 2.
- **Y5 HF receiver**: The two-channel HF receiver front end: two LMH6629 amplifiers (0.69 nV/rtHz, gain 9.9), 50 ohm in and out, from a few kHz to 100 MHz, on one switched 5 V; the host's ADCs sample both coherently (the host's own front end rolls off at 23 MHz; add a 30 MHz low-pass ahead of it for a station beside an FM transmitter).  It carries the Pmod plug in its bottom margin.
- **Y6 VLF loop amplifier**: A VLF / LF loop amplifier, one cell: the loop (or a ferrite rod's coil) on the IN jack through an SMA lead, 1 uF into an OPA810 biased at half the supply by 100 k / 100 k (the loop is a low-impedance source; the 50 ohm termination stays off), gain 101 (10 k / 100 ohm, the 100 ohm to ground through 1 uF: a 1.6 kHz high-pass), 100 pF across 10 k (a 160 kHz roll-off that keeps the AM broadcast band out), 1 uF and 49.9 ohm into OUT.  The LMH6629 tile's core with other values.
- **Y7 reflection bridge**: The reflection bridge, two cells, +-5 V.  DRIVE (the top-left jack) feeds node D; 49.9 ohm from D to A, which runs right along y 4.75 to the DUT jack (the right cell's top-left); 49.9 ohm from D to B and 49.9 ohm from B to ground.  With a 50 ohm DUT the bridge balances (A = B); in general A - B = B x Gamma.  Two OPA810 (SOT-23-5, rot 270) stand under the line, each input a millimetre or two from its node: U1 at gain 2 on B (INC, through 49.9 ohm to the left cell's bottom-right jack) and U2 as a difference amplifier, +IN on A and 499 ohm from U1's output to -IN, 499 ohm feedback: 2 A - 2 B (REFL, through 49.9 ohm along y 10 to the right cell's bottom-right jack).  REFL / INC = Gamma, complex, at every frequency the host makes.  -5 V comes down the back from the gutter above at x 14.6 and 19.8; VS up the back from the switch at x 10.2.
- **Y8 coupled resonators**: Coupled and parametric resonators, two cells, passive: two 10 MHz LC tanks (TDK's 10 uH NLV32, Q about 30), each tuned by a back-to-back pair of BB133 varactors whose cathodes sit at half the supply through 100 k / 100 k.  Like two cavities each has weak capacitive ports: SIG (terminated in 49.9 ohm) drives tank 1 through 2.2 pF, and 2.2 pF from each tank feeds its OUT jack, which the host's 50 ohm ADC input loads (about -43 dB, no buffer, no loading of the tank).  TUNE / PUMP reaches tank 2's varactor cathodes through 100 k (DC: tank 2's frequency, half the open-circuit voltage) and 1 nF (RF: the pump at twice the resonance).  A DIP4 switches 1 pF and 2.2 pF of coupling between the tanks and 2 k of damping across either.  Tank 1 in the left cell's top-right quarter on a bus along y 4.75, tank 2 in the right cell's on y 5.5; the switch under the right jack, its top row fed along y 8.55 - 9.35 from the left and y 9.0 from the right, its bottom row joined to tank 2 round its right side.
- **Y9 noise source**: A switched HF noise source: the base-emitter junction of an MMBT3904 in avalanche (the base at -5 V, the emitter fed from the switched rail VN through 680 ohm: about 8 V across it at 2-3 mA), its noise straight into an LMH6629 at gain 9.9 (the Y5 channel without its input termination) and out through 1 uF and 49.9 ohm.  A TPS22917 load switch makes VN from the tile's 5 V when Pmod IO8 is high: off is the cold state of a Y-factor measurement (the amplifier's own noise), on the hot. The switch and the junction stand in the top-left quarter (no jack there), the amplifier in the top-right; -5 V comes up a finger of L3 from the gutter below.
- **Y10 laser driver**: The laser driver with an RF bias-T, two cells.  SET (terminated, 1 k / 100 nF) drives OPA810 U1 and a 2N7002 that pull SET / 1 k through 1 k from VS: REF = VS - SET.  OPA810 U2 holds the AO3401's source at REF (1 k into -IN, 10 nF to its output, 100 ohm to the gate), so 10 ohm from VS carries SET / 10 ohm: 100 mA per volt, from 0.  The drain (ID) feeds the diode through 330 nH and 27 nH; RF IN reaches it through 100 pF and 47 ohm (about 20 MHz to 3 GHz); an AO3400 from ID to ground (Pmod IO5 high) steals the current for microsecond pulses without upsetting the loop; a B5819W guards against reverse current.  MON is the diode's voltage through 10 k into the host's 50 ohm (1/200).  The RF line drops from RF IN and runs along y 9 to the LD jack's left; the gate and source lines go under it on the back.

## RF-MOD-2: the second-level RF board

11 tiles, 25 jacks, 181 parts.  RF-MOD-2: a coherent I/Q transceiver and microwave physics, rev A.  The second-level RF board, for the ECP5-2ADC-2DAC board: its two DACs drive the I/Q modulator, its two ADCs read the I/Q demodulator, and one synthesizer, split, is the LO of both, so the transmitter and the receiver share one phase.  Around them a low-noise amplifier, a log detector, an absorptive switch, a switched noise source, a GNSS front end and a loop for NV centres' magnetic resonance.  Four layers as RF-MOD: L1 the parts, the drawn wires and a ground pour 0.25 mm from every 50 ohm line; L2 ground; L3 the rails (+5 V everywhere, -5 V in the gutter under row 1 and under X10, the synthesizer's 3.3 V island); L4 ground with the back-side text.  Every jack sits at a cell's top-left or bottom-right corner (the checkerboard of rev A, Prompt 12).  Designed for Prompt 13 ("work on the 2nd version of each board, filling them with whatever would make the most interesting RF (physics and communications) tutorials"; "the second-level boards will almost certainly only be used with the 2DAC+2ADC board").  PLAN_LEVEL2.md section 4 has the reasons.

- **X1 I/Q modulator**: The ADL5375-05 I/Q modulator (LFCSP-24 at (20.0, 9.0), rot 90: the I pair down its left side, the Q pair down its right, RFOUT on top, the LO pins along the bottom).  Each baseband input is single-ended from a DAC (+-1 V into 50 ohm): the jack sees 68 ohm across 150 ohm into a node that 510 ohm from 5 V and 91 ohm to ground hold at 0.50 V, so +-1 V at the jack is +-0.34 V at the pin (0.16-0.84 V, inside the 0-1 V the pins allow); the negative pin of each pair sits at 0.50 V on 510 / 56 ohm (both nodes 50 ohm Thevenin, so the 41 uA bias currents cancel).  LO in through 100 pF to LOIP, LOIN to ground through 100 pF; RFOUT through 100 pF up to the RF OUT jack.  DSOP is grounded (always on).  The tile's switched 5 V (VS) is an island on L3 under the chip and both networks; every VS pin and resistor reaches it with a via.
- **X2 I/Q demodulator**: The ADL5380 I/Q demodulator (LFCSP-24 at (21.6, 11.0), rot 0: RF on top, LO along the bottom, the I outputs down its left side, Q down its right) and two OPA810 difference amplifiers that turn each 2.5 V-common-mode pair into one output around 0 V for the ECP5 board's +-1 V ADCs: 499 ohm into each input, 1 k of feedback and 1 k to ground (gain 2), 49.9 ohm out, so a 50 ohm ADC sees the pair's difference and a high-impedance one twice it.  RF in single-ended to RFIN through 100 pF (RFIP to ground through 100 pF), LO likewise; ADJ on 1.5 k to 5 V (the datasheet's value below 3 GHz); ENBL grounded.  The amplifiers run from the tile's 5 V and the board's -5 V; the 5 V is an island on L3.
- **X3 synthesizer**: The synthesizer: RF-MOD's MAX2871 tile (R10) with its own REF IN, moved to the right cell's bottom corner so the modulator above can use all four of its corners.  It carries the Pmod plug; the switch's two lines (IO5, IO8), the noise source's (IO6) and the modulator's output disable (IO7) leave its other pads along the bottom margin.
- **X4 LNA**: RF-MOD's QPL9547 LNA tile, unchanged.
- **X5 splitter**: RF-MOD's resistive splitter with its jacks on the checkerboard: P1 top-left, P2 the left cell's bottom-right, P3 the right cell's bottom-right.  Three 18 ohm resistors to one node: 6 dB from any port to any other, DC to 6 GHz.  Its job here is X3's OUT A into the LO of X1 and of X2, one phase for both.
- **X6 log detector**: The AD8318 log detector (LFCSP-16 at (14.0, 6.5), rot 90: the input pins on its left, VOUT and VSET on its right, TADJ and VPSO along the top, VPSI along the bottom).  ADI's basic connections: 51 ohm across the input, 1 nF to INHI and from INLO to ground, 499 ohm on TADJ, VSET tied to VOUT (measurement mode, -25 mV/dB), CLPF open (10 ns).  VOUT (0.5-2.1 V) through 110 ohm into 91 ohm to ground: a 50 ohm source, 0.23-0.95 V open (inside the ADC's +-1 V), half that into 50 ohm.
- **X7 noise source**: A switched noise source: the base-emitter junction of an MMBT3904 in avalanche (the base at -5 V, the emitter fed from the switched rail VN through 680 ohm, about 8 V across it at 2-3 mA), its noise through 1 nF into an ADL5611 gain block (22 dB, the 43 nH choke from VN), then 1 nF and a 20 dB pi pad (62 / 240 / 62 ohm) to the OUT jack.  A TPS22917 load switch makes VN from the tile's 5 V when Pmod IO6 is high: off, nothing is biased and OUT is the pad's own 50 ohm at room temperature, the cold state of a Y-factor measurement; on, the hot state.  QOD and CT open: the ADL5611's 94 mA empties VN within microseconds anyway, and the fastest turn-on (about 100 us).
- **X8 GNSS front end**: A GNSS front end for an active antenna: a bias-T on ANT (3.3 V through 10 ohm and 18 nH, for the antenna's own LNA), a 100 pF block, Murata's 1561-1602 MHz SAW, Maxim's MAX2659 LNA (470 pF and 6.8 nH at its input, its own matched, blocked output), a second SAW, OUT.  A TPS7A2033 makes the 3.3 V from the tile's switched 5 V.
- **X9 ODMR loop**: A loop for NV centres' magnetic resonance: the IN line (50 ohm CPW) runs to a one-turn loop 1.6 mm across at the cell's centre and on to the OUT jack, so the current that makes the field is the line's own and nothing resonates: any frequency from DC to 6 GHz, the 2.87 GHz of NV's zero-field splitting and every Zeeman line around it.  No copper on any layer within 2 mm of the loop (a plane under it would carry the image current and cancel the field).  Terminate OUT in 50 ohm, or in a short to double the current (the source sees the reflection).
- **X10 power**: Power: AMOD-A's A1 without its 3.3 V regulator and rails socket.  USB-C on the right edge (5.1 k CC pull-downs), a 2 A PTC, a 5 V TVS, 2 x 10 uF; the LM2664 inverts +5 to -5 V (40 mA) for the demodulator's difference amplifiers and the noise source's junction.  +5 V leaves through a via into the pour that covers L3; -5 V through vias into the island under this tile's bottom half and the gutter strip below row 1.  A green LED on +5 V, a red one on -5 V.
- **X11 switch**: The SKY13370 absorptive SPDT (DFN-6 at (13.5, 8.0), rot 270: V1, RFC, V2 down its left side, RF1, GND, RF2 down its right).  RFC from the top-left jack, RF2 to the left cell's bottom-right jack, RF1 along y 7.5 to the right cell's.  100 pF blocks on every port (the switch wants them); V1 and V2 come from Pmod IO5 and IO8 on the back, 100 pF on each: drive one high and the other low.  The throw that is off is terminated in 50 ohm inside the chip.
